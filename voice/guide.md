# Pipeline-Wide Voice Guide

Read this before drafting anything. All models in the pipeline must follow it.
Brand-specific guides in `brands/<handle>/voice.md` override where they conflict.

## Core principles

- Direct. No buzzwords, no hype, no "excited to share."
- Speak from experience, not theory.
- Short sentences. Concrete nouns. Real numbers when you have them.
- One idea per post. If you need two, write two posts.

## What to avoid

- Generic platitudes that could apply to any industry
- Tool-vendor language ("leverage," "synergy," "best practices")
- Anything that sounds written by a committee
- Posts without a hook in the first line

## Format defaults

- X: one idea, one hook, under 280 characters
- LinkedIn: 3-6 short paragraphs, end with a question or challenge
- YouTube: script with timestamps, title under 60 chars

## How to customize

Copy this file's structure into `brands/<handle>/voice.md` and fill in
brand-specific examples. The pipeline reads the brand file first.
